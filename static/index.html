01 / How It Works

Three deliberate steps designed to get you off the screen
Architecture
Step 01

Listen & Authorize

Connect your Spotify account. Feels fetches your 10 most recently played tracks once per calendar day and caches them in local SQLite. We never poll your history repeatedly.

Step 02

Local Qwen Inference

Your laptop's local qwen2.5-coder:7b model synthesizes tempo, energy level, and emotional texture into a structured outdoor mood profile. Your music never touches a cloud LLM.

Step 03

Grounded Pune Spots

SerpApi queries Google Maps for real, verified places in Pune matching the mood profile. You get genuine trails, lake sanctuaries, and viewpoints with verified ratings and Google Maps directions.

Spotify Setup Required

To authorize with your real Spotify account, add your credentials in .env:

SPOTIFY_CLIENT_ID="your_client_id" SPOTIFY_CLIENT_SECRET="your_client_secret" SPOTIFY_REDIRECT_URI="http://127.0.0.1:8000/auth/callback"

Make sure http://127.0.0.1:8000/auth/callback is added to your Redirect URIs in the Spotify Developer Dashboard.
